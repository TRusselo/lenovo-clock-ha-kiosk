## Findings

1. **A converted clock isn't recognised without `--ip`.** `clock.converted()` needs `ctx.state["ip"]`, so on
   a fresh run the unlock step sent the person to fastboot and the images were rebuilt, although the clock
   was on adb over Wi-Fi the whole time. Only the flash guard caught it. Fix: before the backup, look for an
   adb-over-Wi-Fi device whose `ro.product.device` is `mt8167s_som` (other devices may be connected: a
   Smart Display showed up on USB adb here) and use its address. If there is one, say the clock looks
   converted and offer `lck backup` or `--from <step>` instead of the full setup.
2. **Stopping at the flash question leaves the clock in fastboot,** with no word about it. After "Stopped
   before flashing" (or Ctrl-C there), say: unplug the clock's round power plug and plug it back in to
   start it normally.
3. **The unlock step on an already-unlocked clock** costs a trip to the clock to enter fastboot just to read
   two variables. Once (1) is fixed it should skip itself; until then, say why it's asking.
4. **Minor: the BROM wait shares the job's limit.** `_follow` kills an attempt after `timeout` (4 h by
   default, including the wait), up to `TRIES` (5). In practice nobody hits it, so it's fine as it is.
5. **Time estimate:** the backup took 32 min against "about 40 minutes". Fine as an upper bound.
